Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

SurTaal is an app for learning singing and tabla. It is built to keep your information on your own device. This page explains the few times anything leaves it.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress, practice log, scores, settings, My Songs and Music Studio compositions are saved in your browser or in the app on your device.
  • The microphone is used only while a practice exercise or recording is running. The sound is analysed on your device. It is never uploaded or kept by SurTaal, unless you choose to save a recording as a file yourself.
  • Song files you open in "Sing with your own track" are played on your device and are never uploaded.

If you sign in (optional)

You can use SurTaal without an account. If you sign in, your email address and a copy of your progress are stored with Supabase, the database service SurTaal uses. They are used only to sign you in and to keep your progress the same on all your devices. Only you can read your saved progress.

You can delete your account at any time: Progress → Account & sync → Delete my account. This removes your email address and the progress saved online.

The AI teacher (optional)

When you ask the AI teacher a question, SurTaal sends the following to Google's Gemini service, through SurTaal's server on Cloudflare:

  • your question and the conversation so far;
  • a short summary of your learning: the lesson you are on, your scores in it, your practice minutes and your home note (Sa).

Your name and email address are not sent. Google handles this under its Gemini API terms. On Google's free plan, Google may use what is sent to improve its services, so please do not type personal details into your questions.

YouTube

If you paste a YouTube link, the video plays in YouTube's own player. YouTube's privacy policy applies to it.

Hosting

The app is delivered by Cloudflare and GitHub Pages. Like any website host, they see your connection's IP address when they deliver the app.

What SurTaal never does

No advertising, no tracking or analytics of you, and your information is never sold or shared for marketing.

Children

SurTaal is made for learners of every age. Children should ask a parent or guardian before creating an account or using the AI teacher.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions are welcome through the project page: github.com/sukantasahabim-cyber/Sukanta_APP.
